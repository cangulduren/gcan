Can Güldüren (ORCID: 0000-0002-9048-1228) is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Software Engineering, OSTİM Technical University, Ankara, Türkiye. 

A graduate of the Turkish Military Academy, he studied computer engineering and information technology at Middle East Technical University, holds an MA in Management and Organization from Selçuk University, and an MA and PhD in Computer Education and Instructional Technologies from Ankara University. 

His research interests include software engineering economics, technical debt, explainable AI, multi-criteria decision support and data-driven industrial policy.
